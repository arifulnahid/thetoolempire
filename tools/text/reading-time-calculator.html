What is a reading time calculator?

A reading time calculator estimates how many minutes (or seconds) it takes to read a piece of text, based on its word count and a chosen reading speed. It's the same number you see under blog post titles as "5 min read" — this tool lets you calculate that number yourself for any text, and fine-tune the speed to match your actual audience instead of relying on a fixed default.

The calculation is simple: word count divided by reading speed (in words per minute) gives the time in minutes. This tool also supports a speaking-time mode, which uses realistic spoken-word paces instead of silent-reading paces — useful for estimating how long a script or presentation will actually take to deliver.

How to use the Reading Time Calculator

  1. Paste or type your text into the box on the left.
  2. Your word count, character count, and estimated reading time update instantly.
  3. Drag the speed slider, or tap a preset, to match the reading speed you care about — slow, average, or fast.
  4. Switch to Speaking time if you're estimating how long the text takes to read out loud, such as a speech, podcast script, or presentation.
  5. Use the stats below the time estimate (words, characters, sentences, paragraphs) for any additional context you need, like meeting a length requirement.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is your text's word count divided by a chosen reading speed in words per minute. For example, 600 words at 200 WPM works out to 3 minutes. This tool does that calculation instantly and updates it as you type or adjust the speed.

What reading speed should I use?

200–230 words per minute is a common default for an average adult reading silently on a screen. Use the slower preset for dense or technical text, and the faster preset if your audience tends to skim.

What's the difference between reading time and speaking time?

Reading time estimates silent reading speed, which is faster than speech. Speaking time uses a slower, spoken-word pace (roughly 110–160 words per minute) to estimate how long it takes to read the same text out loud — useful for speeches, scripts, and voiceovers.

Does formatting like headings or bullet points affect the count?

The tool counts all visible words in the pasted text, including headings and list items, since a reader's eyes pass over them too. It does not count HTML tags, markdown symbols, or hidden formatting characters.

Is there a limit to how much text I can paste?

No hard limit — paste a paragraph or a full chapter. Very long text (tens of thousands of words) may take a fraction of a second longer to recalculate, but there's no cap enforced by the tool.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting and calculation happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, logged, or stored anywhere outside your own device.

Can I use this for non-English text?

Word and character counts work for any language that separates words with spaces. Reading-speed benchmarks in the presets are based on English reading research, so treat them as a rough starting point for other languages and adjust the slider as needed.
