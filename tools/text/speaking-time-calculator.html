What is a speaking time calculator?

A speaking time calculator estimates how long it takes to say a piece of text out loud, based on its word count and a realistic spoken-word pace. It's built specifically around speech, not silent reading — people speak noticeably slower than they read to themselves, and a script that looks short on the page can run much longer once you're actually standing in front of an audience.

This tool works in two directions. If you already have a script, paste it in and get an instant time estimate. If you're starting from a time slot instead — a 5-minute toast, a 20-minute conference talk — switch to target-time mode and the tool tells you how many words to aim for.

How to use the Speaking Time Calculator

  1. Choose "I have a script" if you want to time text you've already written, or "I have a target time" if you're writing toward a time limit.
  2. In script mode, paste your text and watch the speaking time update instantly.
  3. In target-time mode, enter your minutes and seconds, and the tool shows the word count to aim for.
  4. Adjust the speaking pace slider, or tap an occasion preset, to match how you actually talk — slower for a eulogy, brisker for a pitch.
  5. Use the word count, page estimate, and sentence/paragraph stats to sanity-check your draft against your time limit before you rehearse.

Frequently asked questions

How is speaking time calculated?

Speaking time is your word count divided by a chosen speaking pace in words per minute. For example, 700 words at 140 WPM comes out to 5 minutes. This tool calculates that instantly and updates it as you type, paste, or adjust the pace slider.

Why is speaking time different from reading time?

Reading silently is faster than speaking out loud, since a speaker has to physically articulate every word and typically adds pauses for emphasis, breath, and audience reaction. A typical silent reading pace is 200–230 words per minute, while a typical speaking pace is closer to 110–160 words per minute.

How many words do I need for a 5-minute speech?

At a conversational pace of around 130–140 words per minute, a 5-minute speech runs roughly 650–700 words. Switch to target-time mode, enter 5 minutes, and adjust the pace slider to get a range tailored to your own delivery style.

Does the calculator account for pauses and audience reaction?

The word-count target range in target-time mode is built with some buffer for natural pauses, but it can't account for planned dramatic pauses, laughs, or applause breaks. For a speech with a lot of intentional pause time, aim toward the lower end of the suggested word range.

What pace should I use for my type of speech?

Use the occasion presets as a starting point — slower for a eulogy or solemn toast, average for a conference talk, faster for an energetic pitch — then fine-tune with the slider based on how you actually speak. Rehearsing out loud with a stopwatch is the most reliable way to confirm your real pace.

Is my script uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting and calculation happens locally in your browser. Your script is never sent to a server, logged, or stored anywhere outside your own device.

Can I use this for non-English scripts?

Word and character counts work for any language that separates words with spaces. The speaking-pace benchmarks are based on English speech research, so treat them as a starting point and adjust the slider to match your own language and delivery.
